Pain point

Growth with no paid ads to fall back on

Many businesses cannot use paid advertising — regulated categories, platform bans, thin margins — yet their survival depends on organic search traffic. When rankings slip, there is no budget lever to pull.

No paid ads allowed

Restricted categories and platform policies rule out Google and Meta ads entirely — organic is the only channel available.

Organic equals revenue

Every ranking position is money. A page buried past position 15 captures well under 1% of clicks — effectively invisible.

Invisible problems

Indexing failures, crawl waste and algorithm shifts go undetected for months — nobody gets an alert when traffic quietly erodes.
